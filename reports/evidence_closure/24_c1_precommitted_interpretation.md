## 1. What C1 can and cannot change

C1 is the project's **post-F1 prospective closure test** for the candidate structural association
`|ACF1|` ↔ interaction sign. It is the only remaining evidence that could change whether that
association is merely sample-specific.

- **It can** upgrade — or permanently retire — the **portability** of that association.
- **It cannot** override Gate A, restore `NS_BENCHMARK_ELIGIBLE`, create an asset-selection rule,
  license routing/gating, or license the dormant NS-inspired operator runner. Whatever C1 shows,
  Gate A's `FAIL` on the structural bridge stands as recorded.

## 2. Branch R — C1 replicates

**Permitted statement (the maximum):**

> On the locked 20-asset Yahoo cohort, under the frozen protocol and with the predictor computed
> strictly before each asset's decision point, the candidate association between `|ACF1|` and the
> interaction effect **replicated prospectively**.

**Also permitted, and required alongside it:** the association remains an **association**. It is not
a mechanism, not a causal claim, not a selection rule, and not evidence that any structural feature
can pick an operator per asset. Gate A's `FAIL` and the downgraded NS eligibility are unchanged.

**Forbidden in this branch:** any wording implying mechanism/causation; any "therefore we can choose
assets/operators by structure"; any restoration of `NS_BENCHMARK_ELIGIBLE`; any claim about
NS-inspired operators; any extension to "post-F1 we should now plan the operator benchmark"
(C1 upgrades portability only — it does not supply the missing mechanism, capacity-matching or
ordinary-convolution/SSM controls).

## 3. Branch F — C1 fails

**Required statement:**

> `|ACF1|` **permanently retires** from the structural-bridge role in this research line.

**What the project keeps** (this is the honest residue, and it is not small):

- the reproduction itself (validated under a frozen protocol);
- the **negative** result on adaptive gating / fusion (8.6 % worse than parameter-free addition);
- **no support** for sample-level routing (12 states × 3 assets × 3 seeds, capacity-matched);
- **F1's narrow conclusion**: asset-level operator preference replicates on a majority of the tested
  assets, **but is not a constant property of an asset** and is materially affected by capacity and
  seed — with the 3/7 instability treated as half the conclusion, not a footnote.

**Forbidden in this branch:** re-searching for another structural feature to replace `|ACF1|`; adding
seeds or assets to rescue the association; re-opening F1; describing the failure as "inconclusive"
rather than a closed line.

## 4. Authorisation record (why C1 runs at all after a FAIL)

The frozen document `16` §3 wrote the FAIL branch as "stop structural-candidate work", with F1 as
the only follow-up. The operator instructed on 2026-09-14 that C1 proceed **after** Gate A = FAIL and
**after** F1 completed. That instruction is recorded here so the audit trail does not read as a
contradiction: C1 is an **operator-approved post-F1 prospective closure test** whose result
**never overrides Gate A**. `16` carries a matching note.

## 5. NS archival wording (binding)

Archive the NS frame as:

> **Not tested and not refuted.** The current evidence does not authorise — or forbid — a
> temporal NS-inspired operator benchmark; the eligibility gate simply does not license one.

**Never** write or imply that "NS was refuted", "the NS hypothesis failed", or "NS does not work in
finance". No experiment in this line has ever been run on an NS-inspired operator.

## 6. Boundaries that hold in *both* branches

- No re-opening of: Gate A, `NS_BENCHMARK_ELIGIBLE`, structural-feature-based asset selection,
  routing/gating, the dormant operator runner.
- No newly added seeds, assets, features, definitions, thresholds or capacity settings.
- The **primary** C1 analysis runs on the new cohort only; the old ten / pooled N=14 appear, if at
  all, as clearly labelled context.
- Raw artefacts are retained; negative results are reported with the same prominence as positive ones.
- Any C1 execution anomaly (missing runs, config/hash mismatch, a predictor that touches the test
  window, incomplete raw artefacts) is an **integrity finding**: it is reported as such and, if it
  requires re-running, the re-run uses the identical configuration idempotently. A stall alone is a
  recoverable operational event, not an integrity finding.
